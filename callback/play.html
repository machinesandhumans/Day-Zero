What they're after

It's rarely about the video. It's about the ask.

💾A downloadA "security update," a "meeting plugin," a "contract to review." One click gives them a door into the network.
🔑Passwords and codes"Read me the code that just came to your phone." "Approve the sign-in prompt so I can get in."
🚪AccessReset a login, add a new phone to someone's account, install a "screen sharing" tool.
📁DataCustomer and patient lists, product designs, payroll and tax forms. And money: wires, invoices, changed bank details.
