You don't have to know it's an attack.

You only have to say something looks wrong. Medical-device plants, labs and offices run on hidden controllers too: cleanroom climate, sample freezers, door badges, building systems.

Equipment acting on its ownA setting changed that nobody touched, or an alarm that doesn't make sense.
Someone asking to open a doorA vendor wants remote access, a password, or to plug something in.
A screen behaving strangelyFiles renaming themselves, pop-ups that vanish. Leave it on and step away.
Report it right away
