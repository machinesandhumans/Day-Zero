What they're up against

Damage control is not a fix

Damage control: one night

  • Dozens of people woken at 2 a.m.
  • Hundreds of phone calls, store by store
  • Heaters switched off by hand
  • Food still lost in stores reached too late
  • The weak point is still there in the morning

Prevention: months earlier

  • A list of every connected device
  • A risk rating: online and out of support goes to the top
  • A budget line to replace it
  • Remote access behind a second sign-in step
  • An alert on setting changes
